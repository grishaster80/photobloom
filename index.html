PhotoBloom

A game made from your photos

Your photos remember more than you do. PhotoBloom turns your camera roll into a daily guessing game — the one quiz nobody else in the world can play.

Three ways to play

Every question is built from your own memories — a sunrise in Kyoto, a coffee in Paris, last autumn's hike.

📍

City Challenge

We show you one of your photos. Where in the world were you? Four cities, one right answer.

🍂

Seasons

Spring, summer, autumn, winter? Read the light, the leaves, the moment — and guess when it was taken.

📅

Date Challenge

Two of your photos, side by side. Which one came first? Easy when they're years apart — surprisingly hard when they're not.

📍 Where in the world were you?

The City Challenge shows you one of your own photos and four possible cities. Was that street café in Paris or Rome? That skyline in Kyoto or Barcelona? Trust the details — and your memory.

🍂 Which season was it?

Spring blossom, summer glare, autumn leaves, winter light. The Seasons Challenge asks you to read the moment in your photo and guess the time of year it was captured.

🔥 Build a daily streak

A fresh challenge every day keeps you coming back. Miss a single day and a once-a-week streak freeze has your back, so one busy day won't reset your run. Watch your best streak climb.

✨ Rediscover every moment

After each round, dive into the photo behind the question — when and where it was taken, and why PhotoBloom picked it. The app quietly surfaces your most beautiful, high-quality shots, so the game always looks gorgeous.

🤖
