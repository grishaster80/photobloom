Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PhotoBloom (the "app") handles your information. The short version: your photos stay on your device and are never uploaded. The app collects a limited amount of anonymous usage data to understand how the app is used and to keep it running well. The detail is below.

1. Your photos

PhotoBloom builds its game from your photo library. To do this it needs read access to your photos, which you grant through the standard iOS permission prompt.

  • Your photos are read on your device only, solely to generate quiz questions.
  • Your photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else.
  • Your photo library is never modified.
  • Photo capture dates and any location data embedded in your photos are used on-device only to build challenges. When a photo contains location data, the app may look up the nearest city name; that lookup sends only a coordinate (never your photo) and the result is cached locally on your device.

You can revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoBloom.

2. Information we collect

We use third-party analytics and messaging tools to understand product usage and deliver notifications. Through these tools, the following may be collected:

  • Usage & interaction data — anonymous events such as which screens you open, which game modes you play, and in-app actions, to help us improve the app.
  • Device & diagnostic data — device model, operating system version, app version, language, and similar technical information.
  • Identifiers — an app-installation identifier and a device advertising identifier, used to measure usage and distinguish devices. These are not linked to your name.
  • Approximate location — a coarse, city/region-level location derived by our analytics providers from your IP address. This is not taken from your photos and is not precise GPS.
  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, a token is used to send you streak and daily-challenge reminders.

We do not collect your photos, the contents of your photos, your name, your email address (unless you choose to email us for support), or your account credentials. PhotoBloom has no user accounts and no sign-in.

3. Third-party services

PhotoBloom uses the following service providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • PostHog (product analytics, hosted in the European Union) — posthog.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase (analytics and push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy

Automatic event capture and session replay are disabled — every analytics event the app sends is one we have explicitly defined.

4. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.
  • To understand which features are used so we can prioritize what to build.
  • To send optional notifications (streak and daily-challenge reminders) if you allow them.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Push notifications

If you grant permission, PhotoBloom sends local reminders to help you keep your streak alive. You can turn these off at any time in Settings → Notifications → PhotoBloom.

6. Data retention

Game data (streaks, scores, and cached city names) is stored on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Anonymous analytics data is retained by our providers according to their standard retention policies. Deleting the app stops all further collection.

7. Children's privacy

PhotoBloom is rated 4+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no targeted advertising.

8. Your choices

  • Revoke photo access in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Limit ad tracking via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device data and stop all collection.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
